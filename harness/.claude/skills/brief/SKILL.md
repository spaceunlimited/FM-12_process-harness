# Consume the brief, then plan

The brief is the student's. You read it; you do not fill it in for them.

Work on the current round: round 1 is `BRIEF.md` → `process/PLAN.md`, round k is `BRIEF-k.md` →
`process/PLAN-k.md`. The session-start line names the round.

1. Read the round's brief. If the student pasted brief text in chat, place it under the right headings
   verbatim, delete the `> (your answer)` line of each section you filled, and confirm what went where.
2. If required sections are still empty, name them in one line and stop. The student writes them.
3. Play the brief back in 3–4 sentences, in your own words: what it is and for whom, the key moment and
   the interaction that carries it, which device, what is faked. Name the biggest risk to what the
   concept says the experience must not cost. In a round 2+ brief, take the carried-over line as settled
   and play back what this round *adds* and what must not change.
4. Ask at most two clarifying questions about what is vague or left out. Wait. Write corrections into
   the brief in the student's words.
5. Write the round's plan from `PLAN-TEMPLATE.md` (in this folder): the key moment in one line, then 3–6
   steps as moments ("they …, and see …"), each with the blocks from `CAPABILITIES.md` it uses and
   fake / scripted / real, and **as the last row the polish pass**. The round is complete when that row
   is done. For every fake or scripted behaviour, name what it stands in for ("scripted (stands in for:
   a model judging the planet)"), so the student knows what the real version would need and can decide
   later whether it is worth it. Plan only what the brief asks for: a block or an asset the brief does
   not mention is not a step, however well it fits; if you think one would serve the key moment, it is
   one of your two questions in step 4, not a row. Show the plan in chat and ask: "Change anything?"
   - Round 1: step 1 is a small slice of the key moment, on the device within 10–20 minutes.
   - Round 2+: the student sizes the rows. A row may be a moment that bundles several changes, so say so in
     the row, and the bundle gets listed and agreed before it is built (rule 3). Numbering starts at 1
     again in this round's table; steps are named `Step k.1`, `Step k.2` in chat, the log and the commits.
6. On yes: append a LOG entry ("round k brief consumed, plan agreed"), then hand over to `/next-step`.

Never: interview section by section · invent content for a section · define workshop terms ·
re-ask what a round brief carried over · build.
