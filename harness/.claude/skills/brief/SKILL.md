# Consume the brief, then plan

The brief is the student's. You read it; you do not fill it in for them.

1. Read `BRIEF.md`. If the student pasted brief text in chat, place it under the right headings
   verbatim, delete the `> (your answer)` line of each section you filled, and confirm what went where.
2. If required sections are still empty, name them in one line and stop. The student writes them.
3. Play the brief back in 3–4 sentences, in your own words: what it is and for whom, the key moment and
   the interaction that carries it, which device, what is faked. Name the biggest risk to what the
   concept says the experience must not cost.
4. Ask at most two clarifying questions about what is vague or left out. Wait. Write corrections into
   `BRIEF.md` in the student's words.
5. Write `process/PLAN.md` from `PLAN-TEMPLATE.md` (in this folder): the key moment in one line, then 3–6
   steps as moments ("they …, and see …"), each with the blocks from `CAPABILITIES.md` it uses and
   fake / scripted / real. For every fake or scripted behaviour, name what it stands in for ("scripted
   (stands in for: a model judging the planet)"), so the student knows what the real version would
   need and can decide later whether it is worth it. Step 1 is a small slice of the key moment, on
   the device within 10–20 minutes. Plan only what the brief asks for: a block or an asset the brief does
   not mention is not a step, however well it fits; if you think one would serve the key moment, it is
   one of your two questions in step 4, not a row. Show the plan in chat and ask: "Change anything?"
6. On yes: append a LOG entry ("brief consumed, plan agreed"), then hand over to `/next-step`.

Never: interview section by section · invent content for a section · define workshop terms · build.
