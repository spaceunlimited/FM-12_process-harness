Status: planned · done · dropped. Changing this table after a test is the point, not a failure.
The last row is always the polish pass: a bundle (rule 3), listed and agreed before it is built, checked
against "what must not change". When every row is done or dropped, the round is complete and the demo is
presentable. Then `/retro`, then `/round` for a brief that extends this one.
Round 1: step 1 is a small slice of the key moment, on the device within 10–20 minutes.
Round 2+: the student sizes the rows; a row may be a moment that bundles several changes.
Numbering starts at 1 in every round's table; a step is called `Step <round>.<#>` in chat, the log and
the commit. A request for something not in the plan is one more row, not a new plan.
